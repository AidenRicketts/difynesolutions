FEATURED WORK

BidPilot: an AI proposal assistant for government contracting

Client engagement: Booz Allen Hamilton, proposal & bid team

CASE STUDY

Turning a 100-page RFP into a bid-ready first pass in minutes, not days.

Booz Allen earns most of its revenue through competitive bids. Every government Request for Proposal means days of reading, compliance checklists and first drafts, and one missed requirement can disqualify a bid.

BidPilot automates that first pass: it extracts every requirement, drafts responses from the firm's own past work, critiques its drafts like a government evaluator, and recommends whether to bid.

Try the Prototype
  1. 1
    Extract requirementsEvery "shall" and "must" pulled into a structured compliance matrix, with no skipped items.
  2. 2
    Draft with company knowledgeResponses grounded in past performance and staff expertise, with sources cited.
  3. 3
    Flag capability gapsRequirements with no matching experience surface early for teaming or hiring decisions.
  4. 4
    Mock government reviewA second AI scores drafts against the evaluation criteria and sends weak sections back for revision.
  5. 5
    Bid / no-bid scoreA consistent, weighted score out of 100 with risks and key dates.
